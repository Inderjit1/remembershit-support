Common questions

How do I put my notes on my lock screen? Tap Save to Photos, open the image in Photos, tap Share, then Use as Wallpaper.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings, then Restore purchases.

How do I cancel? Settings, Manage subscription, or the Settings app, Apple ID, Subscriptions.

Is my data uploaded? No. Your notes are stored on your device only.
