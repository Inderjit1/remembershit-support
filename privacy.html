RememberShit Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

Your notes stay on your device. RememberShit has no accounts and does not upload your notes, walls or wallpapers.

Photos. If you tap Save to Photos, the app asks for permission to add images to your photo library. It can only add images. It cannot read your photos.

Anonymous analytics. We use TelemetryDeck to count anonymous app events such as onboarding completed, wallpaper saved and paywall viewed. These events do not include your notes, are not linked to your identity, and are not used to track you across other apps or sites.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.
